# Weekly SRE orientation

Use current GitHub Actions, pull request, AWS, incident, change, and agreed communication sources.
Separate confirmed evidence from inference and omit unrelated personal content.

## Attention first

List three to five items that must move first. Each item needs an owner, next
action, date, and evidence link or source reference.

## Reliability signals

- SLO/error-budget position and material week-over-week change.
- Open SEV incidents and overdue corrective actions.
- Failed releases, rollbacks, migration drift, or unhealthy deployments.
- Alarm gaps, noisy alerts, database pressure, task-count loss, or outbox age.
- Upcoming maintenance, certificate, credential, backup, and dependency dates.

## Decisions and follow-through

Record the decision, owner, due date, rollback/stop condition, and where evidence
will be captured. Carry unresolved items into the next orientation without
silently changing their status.
